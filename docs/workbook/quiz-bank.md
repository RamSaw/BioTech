# Quiz bank

**Questions only. No answers in this file** — answers here would turn a retrieval tool into a
reading tool, and re-reading is the thing being avoided.

Used at the start of every session (3–5 questions, closed book) and in a larger weekly sweep.
Questions are added at the end of each session. A question answered fluently three times in a row
moves to `## Retired`.

## Open — Phase 1

1. Why would the assays that produced the real negatives have tested almost exclusively 9-mers?
1. Why do the positive peptides peak at length 9?
1. Why should binding depend disproportionately on a few positions rather than all of them? Answer
   from physical structure.
1. Is the 500 nM cutoff a physical constant or a convention? What follows for labels near it?
1. A real binder's letters are shuffled — same amino acids, same length. Should the model still call
   it a binder? What does each answer reveal about what the model learned?
1. Why is a public epitope database overwhelmingly positive, and what is it therefore unsafe to use
   for?
1. Why is the geometric mean, not the arithmetic mean, used to aggregate repeated affinity
   measurements?
1. Why does a random train/test split inflate performance on a peptide dataset?
