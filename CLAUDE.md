# CLAUDE.md

Read these before working in this repo:

- **[`docs/teaching-protocol.md`](docs/teaching-protocol.md)** — how to teach in this project. This
  repo's purpose is learning, not shipping code. Read it before answering any conceptual question or
  starting a learning session; it defines what every session must contain and what must not be done
  for the user.
- **[`docs/TRACK.md`](docs/TRACK.md)** — current phase, next step, and the gates between phases.
  Check which step is active before proposing work.
- **[`development_guide.md`](development_guide.md)** — coding, notebook, linting and dependency
  rules, plus the `[begin]` / `[finish]` protocols.
- **[`docs/decisions/`](docs/decisions/)** — settled decisions on where code, docs, datasets and
  weights live. Do not re-litigate these without reason.

Current state is recorded in [`docs/worklog/`](docs/worklog/) — read the most recent entry at the
start of a session rather than inferring project state from the code.

## At the start of every session

1. Read the most recent `docs/worklog/` entry.
1. **Slack digest.** Summarise conversations in the Bits in Bio Slack worth his attention — job
   posts, intros from people in computational immunology or privacy/ML-for-health, technical threads
   relevant to the current phase. **If there is nothing worth his attention, say "none".** Do not
   pad it; a digest that always finds something becomes noise and stops being read.
1. Check the **Pending** list below and raise anything due.

## Slack rules

- **Never post, reply, react, join, leave, or change anything in Slack without his explicit approval
  for that specific action.** Reading and summarising is pre-approved; every write is not. Approval
  for one message is not approval for the next.
- When drafting something for him to post, show him the text and wait. He sends it, or tells you to.
- The Bits in Bio workspace is a community he does not own. Treat what is read there as other
  people's conversations in a semi-private space: summarise for him, never redistribute, and do not
  quote identifiable people outside this project.
